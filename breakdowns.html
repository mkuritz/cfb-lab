Unit numbers are Sports Labs ratings (0–100 percentile among FBS; higher = better for that unit). Edge = offense percentile minus the opposing defense percentile, after the injury adjustment shown. Injury adjustment: pass edge drops a quarter point per percent of recent catches that are out (15 more if the starting QB is out); rush edge drops a sixth of a point per percent of recent running back carries out. Recent = the team's last three games. "Gives up" ranks are yards allowed per game to each position. Pace is not tracked yet. Injury-adjusted rank is tracking only and is not used in our spreads or prices yet. Newest reported status wins.
