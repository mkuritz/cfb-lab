Sports Labs CFB Power Rankings

Rating = points vs an average FBS team on a neutral field: half opponent-adjusted scoring margin (capped at 28), half opponent-adjusted expected points per play (EPA, offense minus defense, garbage time removed), scaled to points. Def EPA/play is shown so higher = better defense. Talent rk = roster recruiting talent rank (context only, not in the rating). Home edge = 3.2 pts, fitted on 1,905 FBS games from 2024–26 and shrunk toward 2.5. SOS = average rating of opponents. Projections = 10,000 season sims with a 15-pt game sd. Conference title = top two in conference wins meet once (tiebreakers approximated by rating). Playoff odds assume a 12-team field of the five best conference champions plus seven at-large, ranked by a record-plus-strength proxy; it is an estimate, not a committee forecast. Team ratings are Sports Labs ratings (0–100 percentile among FBS), not film grades.
