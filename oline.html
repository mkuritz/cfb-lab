Offensive line analysis

Unit-level line metrics for every FBS team. Ratings are Sports Labs ratings (0–100 percentile, opponent-adjusted), not film grades.

Pass block = opponent-adjusted sack rate allowed. Run block = opponent-adjusted line yards and stuff rate. Hurries allowed = QB hurries credited to opposing defenders in official box scores, per game. Line yards/carry: runs credited 120% of losses, 100% of 0–4 yds, 50% of 5–10, nothing beyond. Power success = short-yardage runs (3rd/4th or goal-line, 2 yds or less to go) converted. 2nd-level yds = rushing yards 5–10 yds past the line, per carry. Havoc allowed = share of the offense's plays ending in a tackle for loss, sack, fumble, interception or pass breakup. Garbage time removed. Individual linemen are not graded: that needs film charting we don't use.
